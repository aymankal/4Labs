Why 4 Labs

Most accelerators want traction. We start before it.

You don't need revenue, a cofounder, or a finished product. You need an idea you care about and the willingness to talk to customers.

1

Mentorship, not lectures

A weekly 1:1 with a mentor who has sold, hired, and built. You bring the problem, we work it together.

2

A plan you can follow

Four labs, two weeks each. Every lab ends with something real: interviews done, a pilot live, a customer signed.

3

Friends in the same boat

A small cohort of founders at your stage. Share wins, get stuck together, and get unstuck faster.

The program

Four labs. Eight weeks. One goal: your first customer.

Each lab is two weeks. You leave each one with a result you can point to.

Weeks 1 to 2
01

Problem

Is this a problem people actually have?

You'll do

  • Pick one clear customer
  • Run 20 to 30 customer interviews
  • Find the pain people would pay to fix

We help with

  • Interview scripts that don't lead the witness
  • Finding people to talk to
  • Reading what the answers really mean
You leave with A validated problem
Weeks 3 to 4
02

Build

What's the smallest thing that solves it?

You'll do

  • Design the simplest version
  • Build it with no-code tools or by hand
  • Put it in front of 5 real users

We help with

  • Cutting scope to what matters
  • Choosing tools you can ship with
  • Testing without writing code
You leave with A working MVP or pilot offer
Weeks 5 to 6
03

Sell

Will someone pay for it?

You'll do

  • Write your first outreach
  • Book and run 10 sales calls
  • Ask for the money, even if it's small

We help with

  • Cold messages that get replies
  • Live call practice and feedback
  • Handling "not now" and "too expensive"
You leave with Your first paying customer or signed pilot
Weeks 7 to 8
04

Pitch

Are you ready to pitch real investors?

You'll do

  • Set your pricing
  • Map your basic unit economics
  • Build a 10-slide deck

We help with

  • Pricing that doesn't undersell you
  • A story built on what you learned
  • Pitch rehearsals until it feels easy
  • Matching you with the right investors
You leave with A pitch and a room full of investors to hear it

Demo Day: pitch to real investors

End of week 8. You pitch live to angels and VCs from the 4 Labs network. We bring the investors, you bring the story. Every founder who finishes gets a slot. After Demo Day we make warm intros to the investors who want to talk.

Who it's for

First-time founders are exactly who we want.

You're a great fit if

  • ✓You have an idea or early prototype, and no revenue yet
  • ✓You're a student, recent grad, or building on the side
  • ✓You're solo or have a cofounder. Both work
  • ✓You want honest feedback, not applause
  • ✓You can give it 5 to 8 hours a week for 8 weeks

Probably not yet if

  • ✕You already have steady revenue
  • ✕You're raising a big round right now
  • ✕You want funding but not mentorship
  • ✕You can't commit to the weekly rhythm

Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. No fee and no equity. Our job is to get you ready and put you in front of investors.

Do I need a cofounder?

No. Solo founders are welcome. If you meet someone in the cohort, even better.

Do I need a product already?

No. An idea you care about is enough. Lab 2 is where you build the first version.

I'm still a student. Can I apply?

Yes. The program is built to fit around classes or a job.

Will I actually meet investors?

Yes. Demo Day is a live pitch to angels and VCs from our network, and we follow up with warm intros to anyone interested. Raising is up to you and the investor, but you'll be in the room.

What if I fall behind?

Tell your mentor. We adjust the plan. The only rule is to keep showing up.

Where is it?

Online. Founders can join from anywhere. Sessions run on Beirut time (UTC+3) and we schedule around the cohort.

Your idea deserves a real shot.

Book a 15-minute intro call, or email a few lines about your idea with "4 Labs" in the subject. No deck needed.
