    Why I do it

    A note to founders.

    I didn't start with a network, a mentor, or a playbook. I started with a phone.

    In my first year of college I took a sales job and learned the slow way. Call. Hear no. Adjust. Call again. By my third year I was leading a sales team while finishing my degree.

    Then I co-founded Vanguard Services in Beirut and built it from zero: the clients, the team, and the partners behind it. Today we run sales and support teams for companies in North America and the GCC.

    Along the way I noticed something. The founders I meet through LvlUp Ventures and Boardy aren't smarter than the young founders I meet at home. They just got access earlier. Someone showed them how to sell. Someone opened the right door.

    4 Labs is how I open that door. Eight weeks, a small group, real mentorship, AI tools, and a room of investors at the end. No fees and no equity. If you're willing to do the work, I'll show you everything I know.
