Who it's for

Built for B2B founders at pre-seed and seed.

Good fit YES

  • You sell to businesses, not consumers
  • Pre-seed or seed, with a product or a clear path to one
  • A founder who can sell, or wants to learn fast
  • Early signal: pilots, LOIs, or first revenue

Not a fit NO

  • Consumer apps and marketplaces
  • Series A and beyond
  • Ideas with no founder commitment
  • Teams looking only for a check
